# Awards for the Union of Theatre Workers of Russia

## Provenance

Selected by the owner for the additional ТРИФОТИ portfolio case. Presented as work from the STEP_3D portfolio, realized by the Technopark RGSU team, not as a claim of original ТРИФОТИ authorship.

- Production article: https://technopark-rgsu.ru/skb/news/nagrady-dlya-zvezd-teatra/
- Official ceremony report: https://stdrf.ru/novosti/sostoyalos-vruchenie-premiy-predsedatelya-i-sopredsedatelya-std-rf/
- Existing public asset repository: https://github.com/STEP3DLab/Technopark_project

The production article confirms digital design and 3D printing by the Technopark RGSU team. The official STD RF report confirms Vladimir Mashkov's role in presenting the awards on 18 December 2025 at the theatre centre “Na Strastnom”. No claim is made about him being a client, coauthor, or endorsing this studio. No unverified lead time, material, dimensions, quantity, or performance result is stated.

# Traveling cup for the RTO Spartakiad

The additional case `rto-traveling-cup` is presented as development and production by Step3D, from the STEP_3D portfolio. It is not attributed to TRIFOTI or to an individual sculptor.

- Existing portfolio case: https://step3dlab.github.io/Technopark_project/industrial-design-lab/cases/rto-traveling-cup/
- Published source: https://github.com/STEP3DLab/Technopark_project/blob/main/industrial-design-lab/cases/rto-traveling-cup/index.html
- Official results: https://stdrf.ru/novosti/itogi-pervoy-spartakiady-rto/

The portfolio describes 3D modeling, printing, assembly, hand finishing and painting. The official results confirm the 2026 event and the overall winner, the Lenkom Mark Zakharov Theatre. The portfolio identifies the theatre as the cup's first holder.

The three unchanged photographs in `dist/assets/projects/rto-traveling-cup/` come from the corresponding public assets in STEP3DLab/Technopark_project:

- `trophy.webp`: completed cup and winner plaque, 1152 × 1536.
- `ceremony.webp`: the cup with the Lenkom team, 1536 × 995.
- `winners.webp`: event participants and winners, 1536 × 954.

Small gallery previews are derived locally; full-resolution images remain unchanged. No photographer is named without a source. Only public portfolio and event information is included.
